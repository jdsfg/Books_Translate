### Chapter Summary

The PhD SOP is the most demanding genre in this book. It is professional academic prose addressed to a committee of faculty who are deciding whether to enter a five-to-seven-year mentoring relationship with the candidate. The 6 Moves operate in academic register: research direction as hook, evidence as specifics, faculty engagement as fit, methodological honesty as restraint.

The 70/30 ratio is the central reality of the form. Most SOPs are formulaic and indistinguishable. The candidate who writes a specific research direction anchored to specific work, who engages with specific recent faculty work, and who acknowledges honestly what they have and have not yet learned writes a document in the 30 percent.

The next chapter (Chapter 7) treats the MBA and professional school essay, where the reader's question shifts again — from research direction to career arc and post-program goal specificity.
