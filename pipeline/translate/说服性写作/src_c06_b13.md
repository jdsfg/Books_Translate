### Chapter Summary

The graduate personal statement is a research document with the candidate behind it, not a personal narrative with intellectual gestures. The reader is Professor Lay on a Saturday morning, looking for a research direction and evidence the candidate is prepared to pursue it.

The 6 Moves recalibrate to the graduate register: Move 2 finds a research story; Move 3's hook is the research question; Move 4's specifics are paradigms, papers, methods, and results; Move 6's AI test is harsher because graduate statements are highly vulnerable to AI generation.

The next chapter (Chapter 6) treats the PhD statement of purpose, where the genre tightens further: the same conventions plus the apprenticeship logic of doctoral admission, where committees are choosing future colleagues and the candidate must signal future-colleague fit.
